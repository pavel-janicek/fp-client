4.1.0 (release 4.1.0):
- New activities are visible only to you by default. Change it in Settings → Privacy; it also applies to your Wear OS watch.
- Your privacy zones show on your activity maps, with a note when a route is hidden from others.
- Settings is now sorted into categories.
- The settings button on your profile is always visible.
- Garmin (experimental, testers): import activities through Garmin Connect. The FP Wear watch app from 4.0.0 is no longer supported.
