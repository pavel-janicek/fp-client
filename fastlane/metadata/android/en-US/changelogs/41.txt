2.2.0 (release 2.2.0):
- Send feedback to the admins of your instance: open the Me tab and tap "Send feedback" — pick a topic, write your message and choose whether the admins may reply to you by e-mail. Feedback stays on your instance.
- Import your workouts from Komoot: Settings → Data → "Import from Komoot". Your Komoot login is used for that one request only and is never stored. The button tells you if your instance has not enabled Komoot support.
- Export your data: Settings → Data → "Export my data" requests a portable archive of your account; when it is ready you get a notification and can download the ZIP from the same screen.
- Change your e-mail address: Settings → Account → "Change email address". A confirmation code is sent to both your old and your new address before the change takes effect.
- Your profile now shows the summits you have reached, with a list of all of them and a detail view per mountain.
- Trim the route of your own activities: open an activity and tap the scissors icon to cut unwanted parts out of the GPS track, with a live preview of the new distance and elevation before you save.
- The activity heatmap on your profile can be enlarged to full screen with one tap and closed the same way.
- Locked (followers-only) profiles now show the person's name and profile picture instead of only a handle, and Edit profile can preview the Gravatar your instance would fall back to if you removed your uploaded picture — looking changes nothing.
- Creating an account now shows which instance you are joining, with a "Change instance" button right on that screen; the e-mail field checks that you really entered an e-mail address, and the form is centered on the screen.
- Fixed a startup crash that could occur after the app was restored from a device backup.
- Fix: on a private account, accepting or rejecting a follow request now dismisses it — the row shows "Accepted" or "Rejected" instead of keeping the Accept/Reject buttons, and a failed response is shown on the row instead of being ignored.
- Fix: "Mark all read" on the Activity tab now also clears the FitPub push notifications from the notification shade — the launcher icon badge counts those, so it stayed until they were dismissed by hand.
